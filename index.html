Setup Support

Fast answers for installation blockers users are likely to hit during a first release.

ELF does not appear as a microphone

Restart the target app after installation, then choose ELF Output from its microphone list.

No sound in the live meter

Confirm the input microphone in ELF and allow microphone access in system privacy settings.

Driver permission blocked

Open system security settings, approve the ELF audio component, and restart the installer.

Need an older build

Archived releases can be linked here once multiple installer versions are available.
